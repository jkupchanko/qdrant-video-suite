On the 41 s Qdrant-brand animation test of Bulk Uploads (2026-10-15) the only critique John relayed was that the transitions and uploads moved a little too fast. Staying on an image or diagram for a while is fine and welcome. Overall he liked it.

**Why:** a viewer needs time to read a graphic; keyword references should land quickly and be understood, but not rush unless many items must be shown.
**How to apply:** transitions about 0.5 s instead of 0.3 s; let a finished diagram hold; cue each element on its spoken keyword but do not stack many changes per second. Next step he asked for: five completely different editing styles copied one-to-one from popular AI YouTube videos, packaged so anyone can hand over a cut + script and get the styled video.
