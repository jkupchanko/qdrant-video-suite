# Camera layout audit: 32 videos, measured

Generated by scripts/audit.py + scripts/tendencies.py on 2026-10-14 from 32 popular AI / software-development videos (Fireship, NetworkChuck, Tech With Tim, IBM, Theo, Sajjaad Khader, Aaron Jack, ForrestKnight, pixegami, TechWorld with Nana, Google Cloud Tech, and others; 2 to 49 minutes). Sampled every 2 s; face detection plus a body test tells a wide shot from a window. Tiny windows under about 5% of the frame width are undercounted, and splits are rare in this corpus, so treat 'half' numbers as thin.


Layouts: full = talking head; half = presenter in a split beside content; inset = large picture-in-picture; small = small picture-in-picture; none = screen only.
