## What the skill recommends

Use content only where reading or detailed inspection needs the space.
Use a small inset when the presenter adds guidance and a reserved region remains clear.
Use a content dominant side panel when a simple view and the presenter both need room.
Treat equal halves as a conditional layout option, not a pattern established by this sample.

The rectangular shape and the top right 20% width preset come from the requested house example.
They are not a measured optimum from the thirty videos.
An observed circle or a different corner does not override the chosen consistent rectangle in our own demonstration.

## Evidence limits

Three saved samples do not establish full video presenter counts, transition timing, cut frequency or the reason viewers stayed.
Frames were assessed inside the actual video rectangle, excluding recommendations and browser controls.
Dimensions are visual estimates, not exact pixel measurements.

One saved foundation sample contains a loading crescent, and some caption and frame alignments are uncertain.
Three coding samples lack timed narration because their available secondary subtitles are untimed.
Those uncertainties are retained in the detailed observations.
No source screenshots or full transcripts are included in this skill.
