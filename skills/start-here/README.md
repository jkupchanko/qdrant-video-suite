# Start here

The front door of the suite. Tell Claude you want to make a video, and this skill walks you through the pathway one
step at a time, with a small map of where you are:

1. **What do you have?** Always asked: a script, and the raw, full recording. A video cannot be made without a recording;
   with a script only you record after it is approved, with only an idea the script is written with you first.
2. **Who is it for, and what should it do?** Always asked: your goal, the viewer, what they know, what they should do
   next, the length, what supports it (docs, articles, repos), your name card. Then a story structure, with the reason.
3. **The script, for that viewer.** Your script reviewed against those answers, or a new one written for them.
4. **Approve the script.** The whole thing, with the B-roll, camera and transitions the skills filled in.
5. **The editing style.** Always asked.
6. **Record**, or hand over the recording you have.
7. **Build**: the raw edit on its own first (takes, joins, zoom; no B-roll or animations), small fixes until you
   approve it; then the visuals, a 60 second preview, and the whole video.
8. **Small edits** until it is ready: each fix re-renders only the seconds it touches, you see just that moment, and a
   final check ends with "ready to go?" Then the last question: **captions?** A subtitle file to upload (recommended),
   the file plus a copy with captions burned in, or none.

You are never asked about the camera, the B-roll or the transitions: you see them in the script and can change them
there.

## Use it

Copy this folder, and the other skill folders, into `~/.claude/skills/`, then say something like:

> I want to make a video. Where do I start?

[`references/script-form.md`](references/script-form.md) is the script's exact form, with the mistakes that have cost a
build.
