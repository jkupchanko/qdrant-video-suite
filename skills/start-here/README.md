# Start here

The front door of the suite. Tell Claude you want to make a video, and this skill walks you through the README's
pathway, one step at a time, saying where you are at each step:

1. **What do you have?** A script, a recording, notes, or just a topic.
2. **Two ways in.** (a) Your script or recording is reviewed: who it is for, what is missing, where the B-roll and camera
   should go, suggested changes. (b) Starting from scratch: explain what you want to teach in your own words.
3. **The script questions.** Who is watching, what they know, the goal, the length, the docs page, your name and title.
   Then one of the ten story structures, with the reason.
4. **The script**, written in full (lines, B-roll, camera calls, transitions, name card), shown to you, and changed until
   you approve it.
5. **The editing style.** Listed Sections, Animated Board or Code Report.
6. **Record**, or hand over the recording you have.
7. **Build** the whole video in one run.
8. **Read the review**, fix what it finds, and build again from that point.

It only asks what your material has not already answered, and it does not move on until each step is settled.

## Use it

Copy this folder, and the other skill folders, into `~/.claude/skills/`, then say something like:

> I want to make a video about hybrid search. Where do I start?

[`references/script-form.md`](references/script-form.md) is the script's exact form, with the mistakes that have cost a
build.
