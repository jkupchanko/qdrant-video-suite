# Transition audit: 50 videos, measured

Generated by scripts/audit_transitions.py + scripts/tendencies.py on 2026-10-14 over 50 popular AI / software-development videos (the 32 from the camera audit plus 18 more: Jeff Su, ByteByteGo, Matt Pocock, Web Dev Simplified, Matthew Berman, Coding with Lewis, Dave Ebbelaar, Traversy Media, Kevin Powell, Nick Chapsas and others). Every frame-to-frame change above the video's own noise was found and classified by what the frames around it do. Limits: 'other' is any whole-frame animated move the tests do not name (a card flying in with motion blur, a graphic wipe, a 3D flip); bright graphic wipes can register as fade-white; punch-ins between two different takes are counted as cuts.


Kinds: cut (one frame to the next), punch-in/out (a cut to the same shot tighter or wider), dissolve (blend), fade-black/white, slide (content moves across), zoom (content scales as a move), other (a whole-frame change that is none of those), element-in/element-anim (a label, card or caption appears over a shot that stays; not a shot change).
