# Upload kit

**Your finished video in; the title, description and chapters for YouTube out.**

When the video is done, YouTube still asks for a title and a description, and chapters only work if they follow
YouTube's rules exactly. This skill writes them from your script, so you never type the same thing twice, and checks
them before you upload.

## What you get

- **Chapters**, one per section of your script, starting where the video really says it, not where the script
  planned it. YouTube's rules are checked: the first at 0:00, at least three, each 10 seconds or more.
- **A description** that opens with what the viewer gets, links the docs in its first lines, lists the chapters, and
  links every page the video shows.
- **Three title options** to choose from, or write your own. Every title is checked: not too long for search, names the
  product, no emoji.

## Checked against 49 popular technical videos

41 of the 49 have chapters, 33 of 49 put a link in the first two lines of the description, and their titles run 28 to
67 characters. The numbers and the reasons: [references/research.md](references/research.md).

## Use it

It runs inside every build, after the review. At the end Claude shows you the description, offers three titles, and
hands you the files with where each one goes. Or just ask: "write the description for this video".

Thumbnails are not made yet: make them by hand for now.
